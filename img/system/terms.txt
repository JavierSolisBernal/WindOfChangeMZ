Please read both - dos and don'ts.

There are two styles I mainly support:

Ace (24*24):
Not more than 2 at one time.

MV Icons (own style, no RTP!) (32*32)
Not more than 2 at one time!


MV Icons II (manually(!) upscaled Ace Style) (32*32)


1 from scratch or up to 2 upscales from my 24*24 icons



Big Icons (48*48):
You can ask for 1 from scratch icon or 2 resizes of existing (in my sheet or the RTP) icons at one time.

(If you want another size, ask me)
Do:
What do you need? - Good description and pictures! If there is no chance to find an image a good description is okay, but a image and just the title isn't!
Be patient and friendly and I'll treat you the same way.
Give that Avy some feedback after she did your request.
Please check the sheet before requesting something.


Don'ts:

No "grocery lists". Write a proper request text!
I won't do recolor requests!

If I refuse your request, deal with it and wait more than just a few days before coming back again.
If there are already a lot of open requests, wait until they are solved.
